GOOD QUESTIONS

Curious?
We get it.

Everything you need to know before exploring a partnership.

Do I have to pay to get started?

Our standard partnership has no upfront store setup fee. Revenue share and any optional services are agreed before launch.

Do I have to make the products myself?

No. We can source and prepare appropriately licensed digital products. You can also discuss products you already own.

Will my store match my personal brand?

Yes. Your storefront is customized around your identity, style, audience, and product collection.

What does Browne's Talent Group handle?

We handle store design, setup, ordinary checkout operations, digital delivery, and agreed ongoing administration.

Can I negotiate a better revenue split?

Yes. The 50/50 example is not a universal requirement. The split can be negotiated in the creator's favour depending on the audience and partnership.

How much could I earn?

Earnings depend on real traffic, conversion, pricing, refunds, and other factors. The calculator uses hypothetical assumptions, not guaranteed or typical results.

Do I need to promote my store?

Yes. Creator promotion is part of the partnership. You introduce relevant products to your audience and disclose the commercial relationship where required.
